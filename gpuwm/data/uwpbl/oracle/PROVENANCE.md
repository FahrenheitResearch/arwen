# UW PBL oracle fixtures of record

WRF v4.7.1 `bl_pbl_physics = 9` (CAMUWPBL), recorded by
`tools/uwpbl_wrf471_oracle` on 2026-09-30.

* Sources: the files `tools/uwpbl_wrf471_oracle/SOURCES.sha256` pins, fetched
  from github.com/wrf-model/WRF at tag v4.7.1 (commit
  f52c197ed39d12e087d02c50f412d90d418f6186) and verified byte for byte before
  compiling.
* Build: the `pristine` variant of `build.sh` (gfortran -O0, WRF's EM
  RWORDSIZE=4 preprocessor defines). `toolchain.txt` records the compiler,
  the C library and the CPU. The `snan` and `spy` variants were built and run
  on the same cases and wrote byte-identical fixtures (no uninitialised local
  reaches an output; the stage recorders are inert).
* Cases: `make_cases.py` with its defaults (seed 20260930, three columns per
  family, four steps) on three grids: `g35` (35 levels, 14 km top, dt 150 s),
  `g44` (44 levels, 16.5 km, dt 60 s), `g61` (61 levels, 20 km, dt 20 s).
  `cases-index.json` names each column's regime family: convective day,
  stable night, stratocumulus-topped marine layer, valley cold pool (half with
  radiation fog), cold mixed-phase column with cloud ice and ice number,
  shallow cumulus.
* `cases-extra35` (index `cases-extra35-index.json`): `make_extra_cases.py`
  (seed 9103042), 48 branch-probe columns on the 35-level grid, two steps,
  which reach caleddy's surface stable-layer, surface radiatively driven
  stratus, low-energy-floor and elevated-CL arms the six families leave
  unvisited.
* Content: `const/` (the physical constants and the saturation table the
  Fortran held) and, per step `s<n>/`, every float32 input camuwpbl received
  (including the carried `kvm3d_in`/`kvh3d_in`/`tauresx2d_in`/`tauresy2d_in`)
  and every output it wrote. Reader: `gpuwm/verify/uwpbl_oracle.py`.

The larger SPY stage records (binary64 arguments of trbintd, caleddy,
exacol, zisocl and every compute_vdiff call, about 100 MB for these cases)
are regenerated by `build.sh` and are not packaged.

`cases-sub35` was recorded on 2026-09-30 with the same pinned,
byte-unmodified WRF sources and pristine gfortran 15.2.0 -O0 / glibc 2.43
build. `make_subnormal_cases.py` (seed 9301735) supplies 28 columns, 35
levels, dt 20 s and two steps: three controls, signed float32 subnormal
input probes (words 00000001, 000116c2, 007fffff and their negative forms
where applicable), qv probes beside the binary64 1.e-30 floor, and normal
qc/qi/qni inputs producing subnormal tendencies. The harness's optional
`.bin.step2` sidecar injects kvm3d, kvh3d and signed residual stresses before
recording step 2, after the scheme's step-1 reset; its NaN retain-value
sentinels are never passed to WRF. The snan and spy output binaries were
byte-identical to pristine, as was o2 on these cases. The packaged binary
is 259,608 bytes, its manifest is 2,696 bytes and the column index is 12,314
bytes (274,618 bytes total); stage records and the generator sidecar are
regenerated rather than packaged. `cases-sub35-index.json` names each probe;
`tests/test_uwpbl_ftz_wrf471_parity.py` pins the fixture hashes and every
measured FTZ mismatch without a tolerance.
