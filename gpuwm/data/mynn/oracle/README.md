The CUDA kernel agrees to 1.0e-4 relative on the four original columns, its
largest departures coming from device FMA contraction inside the cold-branch
saturation polynomial. On the three high-variance columns the measured
relative departures are `sgm` 2.2e-7, `qc_bl` 6.9e-6, `vq` 8.4e-6, `vt`
3.9e-5, `qi_bl` 2.4e-4, and `cldfra` 2.6e-4. `sgm` is two orders of magnitude
*better* there than on the floor-bound columns (5.2e-5), because it now comes
from the recorded `qsq` rather than from `qsat_blend`; but `q1 = (qw -
qsat_tk)/sgm` is correspondingly more sensitive, since `sgm` is about a fifth
of `qsat_tk` instead of a fortieth, so a relative wobble in `qsat_tk` reaches
`q1` magnified rather than divided down. At `high_variance_coarse_grid`
level 12 (t = 227.86 K) the ice saturation polynomial sums Horner terms as
large as 3787 Pa into a 6.97 Pa result - a 543x cancellation - so device FMA
reassociation can move `esi` by ~3e-5 relative; a single -4.4e-5 shift of
`qsat_tk` reproduces the observed CUDA `qi_bl` and `cldfra` at that level
simultaneously. This is FP32 conditioning in the saturation polynomial, not a
defect in the newly exercised sigma path, and the test holds the original
four columns to the original tighter envelope so no regression can hide
behind the high-variance allowance.

The three `high_variance_*` columns were added because the original four
could not discriminate the standard deviation at all. WRF builds sigma as

    sgm = SQRT(max(qsq,0)); sgm = min(sgm, qsat_tk*0.666)
    wt  = max(500 - max(dz-100,0), 0)/500
    sgm = sgm + sgm*0.2*(1-wt)
    sgm = max(sgm, qsat_tk*qpct)

and in the four original columns the final floor won at all 19 computed
levels, so the square root, the `qsat_tk*0.666` clip, and the coarse-`dz`
inflation never reached any output. Setting `qsq` to exactly zero left all
six outputs bitwise unchanged: `sgm` parity pinned the floor and nothing
else. The new columns carry a moisture variance that decays from roughly
1.2-1.55 times saturation at the surface, which puts four levels per column
above the clip and fourteen to fifteen below it but above the floor. Their
grid spacings sit in the three distinct bands of the inflation ramp -
`wt` = 1 (60 m, no inflation), `wt` = 0.6 (300 m, partial), and `wt` = 0
(900 m, the full 20 percent) - so the ramp itself is now observable rather
than merely computed and discarded. They also hand in a nonzero `sgm` at
`kte` (7.5e-4, 1.25e-3, 3.0e-3): WRF's copy-down block assigns `vt`, `vq`,
`qc_bl`, `qi_bl`, and `cldfra` there but deliberately not `sgm`, and with
the original all-zero entry values a port that zeroed `sgm(kte)` passed
identically. The first four columns and their inputs are unchanged and
still appear first and in the same order, so this fixture is a strict
superset of the one it replaces.

behaviour.

`stfunc.csv` pins `phim` and `phih` (`module_bl_mynn.F:7525-7623`) as pure

driver's clamped `[-20, 20]` range plus 13 boundary probes, 408 on the

`a0744d9469687b49dad11fd9d11aa4f34d3293c02099b8311cd1063dda6c3912`;

`f15fe9adb0426ee9d77933002900ddc65937ca9658a71543c1150457472042d5`.

The stable arm is bitwise: it calls only `powf`, and the port routes that
onto the verified glibc transcription in `gpuwm/core/noahmp_libm.py`. The
unstable arm is **not**, and the cause is named rather than guessed:
it calls `atanf`, glibc's `atanf` is not correctly rounded, this tree has
no verified transcription of it, and rounding an FP64 evaluation
disagrees with glibc on a few per cent of arguments. The
`(1 - phi_m)/zet` cancellation that follows amplifies one ULP into tens.

worst case 80 and 84 ULP. Substituting the host `atanf` through `ctypes`
drives all four numbers to zero, which is the evidence for the diagnosis.

registry default for `bl_pbl_physics=5`. Harness SHA-256
`90b54a4a37488b769544b1c3bb8c77c4f556da7eb1b173086000a3b309a2d644`;
the 379,055-byte CSV SHA-256
`f4ebad55d44b66f775cd1262d47d503b229f478c85ca0bc7e4e5c4694441bcaa`.

Result, measured and not rounded off:

- **step 2, the warm start, is bitwise on every field of every column** —
  all 19 profiles the driver writes back, `pblh`, `rmol`, `maxwidth`,
  `maxmf`, `ztop_plume`, and both integer indices;
- step 1 is bitwise on three of the four columns;
- the fourth, `cloudy_deep`, carries an **open residue**. It is still

That residue is not a transcendental floor and is not accounted for. A
standalone Fortran reproduction of the driver body — `get_pblh`,
`scale_aware`, `mym_initialize`, `mym_condensation`, `DMP_mf`,

`cloudy_deep`. So neither the leaves nor the sequencing as the port
implements it can explain it: something the real `mynn_bl_driver` does on
the cold-start path is not being reproduced, and it only surfaces on the
column carrying resolved condensate. The budgets in
`tools/mynn_pbl_wrf461_oracle/validate_driver_oracle.py` are the measured
worst case so a regression still trips; they are not permission.

`driver.csv` is the first fixture in this directory that exercises the
scheme end to end. It still does not admit `bl_pbl_physics=5`: the
runtime selector, the restart identity and the GPU driver are absent.

**Coverage gap: no snow.** `run_driver.F90:101` pins `flag_qs = .false.`
and `:205` zeroes `sqs3d`, so no row of `driver.csv` carries a snow
mixing ratio. That is why the port's `qs -> kzero` substitution at the
`mym_condensation` call cannot be replaced by the real `sqs` under this
fixture: there is nothing to check the plumbing against. WRF sets
`FLAG_QS` for `mp_physics` 6, 8, 10 and 18, so this is a gap against every
snow-bearing microphysics rather than against an exotic option. The
deviation it leaves is measured — against WRF's own `condensation.csv`
row, which *does* carry snow — in
`tests/test_mynn_pbl.py::test_the_withheld_snow_species_deviation_is_bounded`,
and the restriction is published in the physics registry. Closing it needs
one new driver-oracle case with `flag_qs = .true.` and a nonzero `sqs3d`;
adding the plumbing first would be a fidelity claim with no oracle behind
it.

m/s, which is what binds davis' `ZW` knee at u* = 1.06, both Taylor-Yelland
clamps, and both garratt clamps. `control_land` and `control_snow_land` are
negative controls that must not move with `ISFTCFLX` at all.
`xland_exactly_1p5` sits on the boundary the module tests three different
ways: `:625` is `.GE. 0` so it takes the water roughness, `:1065`/`:1073` are
`.GT.`/`.LT.` so neither HFX arm fires and its HFX comes back exactly as it
went in, and `garratt_1992`'s own `landsea-1.5 .GT. 0` sends it to that leaf's
LAND arm. All three are asserted.

The leaf sweep is 32 samples through each of the seven roughness leaves, plus
`garratt_1992` at landsea 2.0, 1.5 and 1.0. The gpuwm CPU reference is bitwise
identical to the unmodified module on all 288 leaf rows on all three measured
platforms, because those leaves route `exp`/`log`/`**` through
`gpuwm/core/noahmp_libm.py` instead of NumPy. The whole-column comparison is
not bitwise and carries the measured three-platform union in
`tests/test_mynn_surface_water.py`; what is left in it is the psi tables and
the saturation `exp`, which are still NumPy and are shared with land.
