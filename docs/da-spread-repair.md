# WOOF spread repair, tier 1

The selectable cycle door is `tools/da_cycle_prepared.py --spread-repair innovation`. It replaces the older blanket and echo-only noise arms for that run. Threshold arms use `--spread-repair-z-threshold 25` or `35`; RTPS arms use `--rtps-alpha 1.08` or `1.20`. The same fields propagate through `plan_radar_assimilation` into `RadarAssimilationConfig`, so da-tune's `[recipe.args]` runs the component at its direct assimilation seam. The `blanket` policy is a labelled comparison arm. New storm-spread controls are not promoted until the S1/S4/S5/C4 sheets pass the design gates.

Noise is generated on the compute array backend, smoothed without periodic boundaries, normalized before tapering, recentred across members, then masked cell by cell. Strong echo requires observed Z above the selected threshold and positive mean innovation above 10 dBZ. Missed echo requires observed Z above 15 dBZ and every member below 1 dBZ. Wind, temperature and vapour amplitudes are 0.5 m/s, 0.5 K and 5 percent. Hydrometeor mass is not seeded. Exactly dry members need a forecast leg to turn thermodynamic spread into hydrometeor covariance. No covered clear or uncovered cell receives noise in the innovation policy. Staggered faces require echo on both adjacent mass cells.

`gpuwm.da.spread_repair.SpreadRepairController` also supplies spatial inverse-gamma inflation state. Its enhanced finite-ensemble innovation likelihood includes observation error and localization weight. Log-space quadrature projects posterior moments back onto an inverse-gamma distribution. This is a numerical variant of enhanced inverse-gamma inflation, not DART byte parity. Inflation is bounded when applied; square-root damping occurs before the next prior is inflated. A callback can reevaluate H(x). The cycle adapter rescales H(x) and model anomalies together, the linearized covariance approximation, and stamps that choice in its receipt. Bounds reduce a whole anomaly vector rather than clip members and add water.

`--spread-repair-adaptive` carries the controller's posterior shape, scale and cycle counter with each accepted ensemble generation. Rust writes the float64 arrays before the generation manifest commits. The sidecar binds configuration, seed, observation grid, prepared identity, generation clock and last analysis UTC. Resume refuses missing or changed uncertainty. Verification-only analyses fork the controller; failed positivity and capacity retries do not double-learn. With the flag absent, the generation writer adds no adaptive metadata. CPU tests exercise real Rust writes and continuation through the next analysis. Weather qualification remains pending.

`--spread-repair-vtsm --spread-vts-ram-gib G` wires actual minus 15, zero and plus 15 minute native retention into member legs. The future slot uses a real unanalysed continuation and restores the complete central native tree before publishing the member result. Sealed anonymous RAM preserves numeric dtype across packed workers. The roster barrier checks every member, native clock, geometry, common forecast origin and observation cutoff before pooling 3K covariance samples. Radar, surface, conventional and GOES observers consume the actual retained slots. The cycle returns K central trajectories, recentres on the pooled posterior mean and reapplies its configured water bounds. Current-volume TTEN, insufficient LBC horizon and absent RAM admission are refused. CPU tests exercise contracts and real observation operators; the native producer byte-identity probe and weather qualification are reported separately in the lane hand-off.

VTSM uses captured nonlinear H(x) for its original states and the existing, explicitly named linearized observation-anomaly formulation for inflation. The radar TargetGrid remains the placement and localization-height authority; this change does not add a new physical vertical reinterpolator. VTSP has numerical API tests but no selectable cycle door: arbitrary recentered surface states require a general native nonlinear surface rediagnosis observer, which the current Noah-only post-insertion refresh cannot provide. Host RAM admission must include the central reduction's temporary 3K float64 prior/posterior and K output as well as the retained payload.

The AR(1) boundary fallback was already in the base. Its boundary series preserves the relaxation width and member-specific wind, temperature and vapour tables. Real member boundaries and retiring the initial shared-boundary rim taper belong to the later boundary lane; this fallback does not establish them.

Picture qualification must use native REFL_10CM and the Rust renderer: S1 five columns, S4 Z and vapour spread, S5 stamps and paintballs, C4 consistency and ranks. Look at these before scores. The 2026-10-01 18Z replay and 12-cycle confirmation remain necessary; synthetic numerical tests establish no weather skill. The binding 03:30Z stop invalidates earlier reflectivity/precipitation proof attempts and requires lane 0's fixed-model test before new weather runs. The repair, adaptive and VTSM controls remain named options, default off. The periodic vertical-wrap correctness fix is default on and retires its obsolete quarter-column guard.
