Each class has 88 passes and 26 existing-input refusals.

The complete matrix is `work/research-catalog-cpu-matrix-20260906T091910Z/combined-matrix.json` under the engine checkout. It preserves the two audited frozen batches, their source inventories and the compatibility audit for their intervening changes. The catalog and policy hashes remained identical within those batches. Final release formatting changed only CRLF to LF in the catalog and the new legacy-workflow Rust module; retained before/after bytes prove identical parsed catalog content and no other edits (`research-final-line-endings/receipt.json` in the task workspace). The 26 refusals per class are 23 archived-parent studies and three supplied tropical-state studies; they require actual existing input and are not ordinary fresh-configuration successes. The detailed catalog audit and raw receipts are retained as internal development evidence and are not distributed in the public snapshot. The [public research guide](../public/RESEARCH-WORKSPACES.md) describes the catalog and its method limits.

These rows establish creation and CPU admission, not 570 completed weather forecasts. Method execution evidence is listed separately below. An observed failure remains part of its receipt and is not converted to a pass by a later successful run.
