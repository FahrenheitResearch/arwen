# ArWen 2.7.6 development record

The full landing detail behind ArWen 2.7.6, kept in full. CHANGELOG.md carries the release note; this page carries the mechanism behind each of its rows. Two words of the landing rows were replaced on the way here: the render code paths are called routes, and the importer's re-import is called a read-back; one bold heading that stood twice, the second time with no rows under it, stands once; nothing else changed. Of 2.7.5's four known limits, the rw-wps bundle's is closed by this release, two were re-checked against this tree and restated (`gpuwm cycle` draws nothing while it runs; the nowcast score masks a 9 km rim and scores one member), and the desktop map's ICON global limit is restated as 2.7.5 stated it, because the desktop's source is not in this tree and it was not re-checked here.

## 2.7.6 (unreleased)

New:
**Speed and numerics, measured on an RTX 5070 Ti**
- Omega (WRF `calc_ww_cp`) is diagnosed one column per thread in the Fortran's own operation order instead of through a CuPy tree reduction and batched scan, whose scan alone cost about 1.2 ms per call at 480x384x49. A whole step is 5.1 to 7.0 percent cheaper: 1.07, 2.02 and 5.33 ms per step at 250x200x49, 320x256x49 and 480x384x49 under Morrison. The summation order changed with it: one call of Omega moves by at most 6.0e-7 of its maximum (6.5 ULP of the field maximum), the dynamic fields by 1.7e-4 to 7.2e-4 of theirs after 60 steps, no moisture mass field or number moment moves except Morrison's cloud droplet number (2.5e-6 of its maximum); the phase-2 step capture was re-pinned with those readings. Proposed and measured by weiserhase in issue #5.

**The nowcast front door replays an archived window**
- `python -m tools.da_nowcast run --window-end <ISO>` is about that hour, survey included. The survey lists the volumes, measures the lag, censuses the echo and derives the storm motion that sites the domain, and it now asks the archive about the window end instead of the wall clock. `--window-end latest` is the live case and the only one that reads the wall clock, because it is the only one with no window yet.
- The fine nest over the free forecast is reachable from that door: `--nest-half-width-km` (or `--nest-nx`/`--nest-ny`) turns it on exactly as it does on the cycle driver, and `--nest-ratio`, `--nest-members`, `--nest-i-parent-start`, `--nest-j-parent-start`, `--nest-history-interval-s` and `--nest-acknowledge` are forwarded unchanged to the driver that owns it. What it costs is not stated yet: the one pair run so far had its two arms meet different card loads, so a back-to-back pair on an idle card is owed before a number is quoted.
- `--source hrrr` defaults to `thompson-mp8-ysu-mm5-noah-rte-rrtmgp-v1`: the suite it has always run, Thompson mp8 with YSU, the classic MM5 surface layer, Noah and no cumulus, now with RTE+RRTMGP longwave and shortwave in place of the legacy RRTMG pair. Every route defaults to the same radiation engines, so which model initializes a forecast no longer decides which radiation solver integrates it. The legacy arm is untouched and still selectable by name, `--physics-profile thompson-mp8-ysu-mm5-noah-rrtmg-legacy-v1`, on every door that offered it before; the two profiles carry identical components and identical settings apart from the radiation engine. It also buys domain. Measured with `gpuwm domain --source hrrr --ladder 12-3 --hours 6 --card 16gb`, once bare and once naming the legacy profile: the default fits 268x216 at 12 km with a 536x432 nest at 3 km, priced at a 13.76 GiB peak forecast envelope, against 184x146 and 368x288 priced at 13.74 GiB, which is 2.15 times the root cells for the same card and the same envelope to within 0.02 GiB. The storm nowcast's default follows the route's, as it has since 1.8.8, and so does the suite an imported case catalog stamps on a case that names no profile; a proposal that types the radiation engine by switch still composes, because a typed `ra_rrtmg_variant` now carries the `wrf_rrtmg_compatibility` token that records it instead of being refused against the one the profile wrote. Both 4/4 engines still bound a longwave column at 128 radiation layers, so a deep hand-authored ladder is refused at the same depth on either arm.
- `gpuwm render --products isotherm_height_0c,isotherm_height_minus10c,isotherm_height_minus20c` draws the height of an isotherm above sea level: the lowest crossing above the ground, interpolated between the two model levels that bracket it, and no colour where the column never reaches that temperature. The wrfout import plans and writes the three planes from its native columns (rw-wrfbatch `wrf_column_planes.rs`), `rw_wrfbatch --list-products` lists the rows, and the planned store field `wrf_freezing_level` that no product drew is superseded by them. The map path drew the first version of these rows blank: the rule that keeps an isobaric geopotential height as a contour analysis (heights as contours, colour reserved for the wind) caught every non-surface member of the field, the isotherm's included, so a 0 C surface that crossed every column of a real 12 km frame between 333 and 5,112 m came out as decametre contours on an empty map; the rule now exempts the isotherm selector as it exempts orography.
- `supercooled_water_path`, `supercooled_water_path_0_3km` and `supercooled_water_path_3_6km` draw the cloud liquid water in a column colder than 0 C, in g m-2, integrated with the model's own dry-air mass per level, (C1H MU + C2H)(-DNW)/g, the measure the cloud water path operator and the conservation case already use; a file without the hybrid coefficients is on the terrain-following coordinate, where C1H is one and C2H is zero, and a layer row counts the model levels whose height above ground lies in the layer. This tree's own history stream writes the full eta levels `ZNW` and no `DNW`, so the first version skipped every plane that integrates over a layer mass with a note and `--list-products` answered the rows with "not stored" on the tree's own forecast; the import now takes each thickness as the difference of the two full levels a layer sits between, and refuses naming both variables only when a file carries neither. A stored-plane fixture written the way the history stream writes it pins the integral: 244.65 g m-2 in the one cold layer, zero in the layer below it, the interpolated 0 C crossing to within a metre, NaN where no level is cold enough.
- `cloud_water_column_max`, `rain_water_column_max`, `cloud_ice_column_max`, `snow_column_max` and `graupel_column_max` draw the column maximum of each hydrometeor mixing ratio on one fixed bar, 0.01 to 5 g kg-1, so a condensate map is a named product on a settled scale instead of `var:wrf_qcloud` on an auto-ranged ramp. A scheme that carries no graupel gets a note, not a failed import. The planes are stored in kg kg-1 and drawn in grams through the same units seam the `var:` and `mesh:` routes use.
- `simulated_ir_satellite` draws from a wrfout. Its row asked for a stored selector no import wrote; the wrfout import now derives a brightness temperature by integrating cloud optical depth downward from the model top (0.145 m2 kg-1 for cloud water, 0.272 for cloud ice plus snow, on the same dry-air mass per level), the temperature at the level where the depth reaches one, and the skin temperature where the whole column stays thinner than that.
- A vertical cut's fill term takes its level list as the colour bar's range: `xsec:QCLOUD=0.01,0.1` draws every frame of a series on 0.01 to 0.1 g kg-1, the receipt says `rule=named`, and a frame with no signal keeps that bar instead of a 0 to 1 placeholder. The list was parsed and then read only on overlays.

- A run whose terrain the configured vertical coordinate cannot order now derives the coordinate that can, instead of stopping at the prepare stage and printing the arithmetic. `etac` becomes the largest value WRF's cubic hybrid supports for the highest ground the run can touch: every declared domain's terrain at its own resolution, and for a following nest the whole statics corridor it may traverse. The model top is untouched. The refusal survives for terrain no positive `etac` orders. Reported against a shipped cyclone preset placed over a bay whose 12 km parent reaches the world's highest terrain.
- An ERA5 window sized for a 16 GiB card initializes and runs. It refused with "specific humidity must be finite in [0, 1)" while the same centre sized for an 8 GiB card ran end to end, and nothing in the forcing was out of range: the refused values were made by the overlapping-parabolic operator that maps the source onto the model grid, and whether a window met a cell where that operator undershoots is a question of how much ground it covers. The check now judges the mapped field against that operator's own envelope for the source it mapped, admitting everything down to it and refusing anything past it, instead of refusing every negative value on the direct specific-humidity route. The envelope is the operator's two-sided bound: -9/32 of the source maximum, lowered by 1 + 9/32 of the source minimum where that minimum is negative, so a source carrying a small negative of its own lowers the envelope by what the operator can do to that value rather than being refused at the mapping, and a source whose minimum is positive gets -9/32 of the maximum alone. Every admitted value is already floored at WRF's qv_min before it is published.
- `gpuwm check` admits the forcing `gpuwm run` runs. ERA5 publishes its pressure levels descending, deepest first, and the check door refused that ordering before it reached its memory section while the run door normalized it and ran the same file end to end. One admission now answers the ordering for both doors, and the p_top coverage and terrain-column tests read the top level and the deepest one instead of the first and last records of a container.

Before, the packaged mapping declared none of them, so every run through the route began dry while `--source hrrr` carried all five, and the parabola would have put a ring of negative mixing ratio around each compact cloud (120 negative cells from one cloudy source cell on a 21x21 grid, none with the bilinear owner). The five rows are table work in the packaged mapping (GRIB2 discipline 0, category 1, parameters 22, 82, 24, 25 and 32 on pressure levels), the regular-source horizontal pass routes them to the bilinear owner by name, the atmospheric window crops them on both sides of the regular join, the mapped-engine decode and compose goldens were re-measured on the new mapping digest, and the prepared cache's `hydrometeor_initialization` receipt names the operator per species. On one cloudy window the decoded maxima were cloud water 3.68e-3, cloud ice 1.64e-4, rain 2.86e-3, snow 5.10e-3 and graupel 5.33e-3 kg kg-1. Vertical velocity still starts at zero on every route, as stock `real.exe` leaves it, and the receipt says so. - `tests/test_sase_gpu.py::test_sase_driver_additive_switch_is_selectable_and_fires` compares `sase_additive_dissipation=true` against an explicit `false` instead of against a bare configuration. The bare configuration has carried the channel since the default became `true`, so the test compared the default against itself and reported a wired switch as not wired. On an RTX 4090 under NVRTC 13.3.33 the switch moves 240 of 1,920 subgrid-energy cells on the test's stably stratified columns (largest decrease 6.75e-07 m2 s-2), an explicit `true` is bitwise the bare default, and no model bytes changed. - A reflectivity or hydrometeor analysis runs under Thompson. `gpuwm.da.moments` had no registered repair authority for mp 8 or mp 28, so a cell the analysis gave mass and no number moment was a refusal rather than a repair, and one real cycle refused with 1,834 such cells. The repair is now the scheme's own entry moment-consistency block (`module_mp_thompson.F:1827-1899`), mirrored on the host in `gpuwm.verify.thompson_entry` and reached through the physics registry's own row: mass with no number takes a 100 um droplet, a 5 um crystal capped at 999e3 per cubic metre, or a 1 mm median volume drop, and a number left standing over mass the scheme calls absent is zeroed with it. Where two registered schemes spell the same moments and repair them differently, the analysis asks for `mp_physics` instead of guessing.
- The positivity policy holds an opinion about every number concentration the prognostic contract carries. The aerosol tracers `nwfa` and `nifa`, Milbrandt-Yau's hail number and WDM6's CCN number joined that contract after the policy's list was written, so a filter increment drove one of them to -1.005e8 per kilogram and the next leg's health gate refused the state. The two lists live in different files, and when they disagreed the run found out four minutes later in another process. The policy is read from the installed module, so a tree whose list has grown past the package a run imports is caught before the card is spent. The merged increment is also bounded on every leg and over every field, because that mapping is what the saved generation carries.
- The hot start's insertion and the filter's increment are bounded as one analysis. Each half was admissible against the background on its own and nothing bounded their sum, so a cycle put water vapour at -1.02e-4 kg/kg and the following leg refused it.
- The analysis increment is conditioned so it cannot hand the next leg a broken moment pair. Where the background holds a species with a positive number, the number takes the same factor the mass took; where it holds none, the cell declines the mass increment and keeps the background. Only cells that would otherwise break are touched.
- The correction carried down to a nested child is bounded by the run's own positivity policy against the child's background. The parent's bound was computed against a different field, and a nested leg was handed water vapour at -1.014e-5 kg/kg on its boundary row.
- The 1 km child runs on every cycling leg and is corrected by its parent's analysis instead of being rebuilt from it. Attached to the free legs alone it was born at the fork and lived 90 to 165 seconds before the analysis was handed on, so any window compared across that handoff measured the birth as much as the weather. A ladder refined to 200 m layers under the 15 s step chosen for 680 m ones ran the limiter at 1.41 on 606 cells and halved the storm's peak updraft from 33.9 to 18.5 m/s.

- `tests/test_sase.py::test_sase_workspace_joins_the_domain_estimate_as_step_transient` reads the closure's resident additions off the domain estimate's own itemization and names them, instead of comparing them against one field's worth of bytes. The check now requires the added resident set to be exactly the subgrid energy and that tendency, requires every other resident item to be byte-identical with the closure off, and prices each of the two against the inventory that owns it.
